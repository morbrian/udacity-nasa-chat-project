## 🚀 Apollo 11 Crew Biographies

> **Source:** [NASA Mission: Apollo 11](https://www.nasa.gov/mission/apollo-11/)

---

### **Neil A. Armstrong**
*Commander*

As the **first human to set foot on the Moon**, Armstrong’s legacy is monumental. He began his career in 1955 as an aeronautical research scientist with the NACA (NASA’s predecessor).

* **Selected:** Astronaut Group 2 (1962).
* **Previous Flight:** Gemini 8 (1966).
* **Role:** Commanded the Apollo 11 mission and piloted the Lunar Module *Eagle* to the surface.

---

### **Edwin “Buzz” Aldrin**
*Lunar Module Pilot*

Dr. Aldrin was a highly decorated fighter pilot in the Korean War and an engineer before becoming the **second human to walk on the lunar surface**.

* **Selected:** Astronaut Group 3 (1963).
* **Previous Flight:** Gemini XII (1966).
* **Role:** Piloted the Lunar Module and performed the mission's EVA (Extravehicular Activity) alongside Armstrong.

---

### **Michael Collins**
*Command Module Pilot*

Often called the "loneliest man in history" during the mission, Collins remained in lunar orbit to ensure his crewmates had a way home. He logged a total of **266 hours in space**.

* **Selected:** Astronaut Group 3 (1963).
* **Previous Flight:** Gemini X (1966).
* **Role:** Piloted the Command Module *Columbia* in orbit around the Moon.

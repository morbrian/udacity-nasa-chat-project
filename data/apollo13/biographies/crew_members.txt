## Apollo 13 Crew Biographies

> **Source:** [NASA Mission: Apollo 13](https://www.nasa.gov/mission/apollo-13/)

---

### **James A. Lovell, Jr.**

**Role:** Commander

* **Selected:** September 1962.
* **Key Experience:**
* Backup Pilot: Gemini 4.
* Backup Commander: Gemini 9.
* Backup Commander: Apollo 11 (for Neil Armstrong).


* **Legacy:** Retired from both the U.S. Navy and the space program on March 1, 1973.

---

### **John “Jack” Swigert**

**Role:** Command Module Pilot

* **Selected:** April 1966.
* **Key Experience:**
* Support Crew: Apollo 7.
* Originally assigned to the Apollo 13 backup crew; famously stepped in to replace Thomas K. Mattingly.


* **Flight Time:** Logged **142 hours and 54 minutes** in space during his first flight.

---

### **Fred Haise**

**Role:** Lunar Module Pilot

* **Selected:** April 1966.
* **Key Experience:**
* Backup Lunar Module Pilot: Apollo 8 and Apollo 11.
* Backup Spacecraft Commander: Apollo 16.


* **Flight Time:** Logged **142 hours and 54 minutes** in space.
